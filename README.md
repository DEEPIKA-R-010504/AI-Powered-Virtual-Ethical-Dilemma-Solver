Ethical dilemmas are complex situations that require individuals to make challenging decisions, often involving conflicting values, priorities, or stakeholders. 

Such scenarios can arise in various domains, including healthcare, law, business, finance and artificial intelligence development.

Ethical dilemmas in banking and finance arise when institutions face conflicts between profitability, regulatory compliance, and ethical considerations. 

So,this model is build to overcome such dilemmas and to get advise and a solution for the dilemmas.
